# Product photograph credits

Every picture in this directory is a real photograph obtained under a licence that
needs no permission and imposes no condition this repository cannot meet. They were
downloaded once and committed; nothing is fetched at run time, so no link can rot and
the seeder needs no network.

**No image here came from an online marketplace.** The dataset this replaced hotlinked
a marketplace CDN, which was both a copyright problem and a dependency the other side
could switch off — and did.

## Summary

- 92 photographs across 40 product subjects
- 72 under **CC0**
- 20 under **Public domain**
- Source: Wikimedia Commons, including the Unsplash archive donated to Commons under CC0
- Processing: centre-cropped to a square and scaled to 640x640, re-encoded as JPEG.
  Cropping and scaling only; nothing was composited, retouched or recoloured.

CC0 1.0 waives copyright entirely and requires no attribution. Public-domain works
carry no copyright at all. The credits below are therefore given as a courtesy and for
traceability, not because a licence compels them.

Machine-readable form: `manifest.json`, which is what the seeder itself reads.
